# How to Apply

Please follow the "Admissions" link at the [IIT Delhi main webpage](https://home.iitd.ac.in/) to apply to our academic programs.

**PhD**: Applications are accepted throughout the year. If the portal is closed, ask the SIT Office for a PDF form that you can fill and send. Admission is a 2-stage process: (1) Shortlisting, followed by (2) Interviews.

**MS(R)**: Applications are accepted twice a year. Admission is a 2-stage process: (1) Shortlisting, followed by (2) Interviews.

**M. Tech.**: Applications are accepted once a year. Admission is managed through the COAP portal.
