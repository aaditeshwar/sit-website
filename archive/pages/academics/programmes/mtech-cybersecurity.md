# M. Tech. in Cyber Security

SIT hosts the interdisciplinary **M. Tech. program in Cyber Security**, for which the first batch of students was admitted in 2021. Partner academic units in this program include Departments of Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, and Management Studies.

The  [CoE-CSIA](https://csia.iitd.ac.in/) co-ordinates the  Cyber Security initiatives in the institute.
