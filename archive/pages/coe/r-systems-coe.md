# R-Systems CoE

**R-Systems CoE for Applied AI for Sustainable Systems**

The CoE focuses on the use of AI and data-centric strategies to tackle real world problems. It aims to facilitate the development of responsible and sustainable computational and socio-economic systems, all bolstered by cutting edge information technologies and AI.

Operationally, there will be a committee consisting of a few ANSKSIT faculty members, one representative from R-Systems, and external expert(s) which will seek and select project proposals from ANSKSIT faculty members (and other collaborators) under the broad scope of the CoE at regular intervals (at least once in a year). We expect to support one or more research projects each year for the duration of the CoE (5 years).
