# Batch of 1969 Innovation Fellow Award 2022

Ms. Subhani (PhD scholar at Amarnath and Shashi Khosla School of IT) under the guidance of Dr. Sorav Bansal (Associate Professor, Microsoft Chair at the CS department) has received the “**Batch of 1969 Innovation Fellow Award 2022**” for the project titled, “**Certified Compiler and Deep Semantic Code Analyzer based on AI techniques**”. This award recognizes and supports their entrepreneurial effort (CompilerAI Labs) of building a product prototype from their research exposure. Please visit <https://compiler.ai/> to know more about their work.
