# Announcement

*We are looking for students at all levels (PhD/Masters/Bachelors), research assistants, post-doc fellows, and software engineering professionals with 0-8 years of experience, to build the [CoRE Stack](https://core-stack.org/), an extremely ambitious and impactful digital public infrastructure for climate change adaptation for rural communities. Please see this list of [ongoing projects](https://www.cse.iitd.ernet.in/~aseth/ongoing-projects-24-25.html) to get a sense of the work and corresponding skillsets. Some ideas for new PhD topics are [here](https://www.cse.iitd.ernet.in/~aseth/phd-topics-24-25.html). Interested students can email me their CVs and also read this [note on our research philosophy](https://www.cse.iitd.ernet.in/~aseth/research-at-act4d.pdf). Interested research assistants, please read this [job description](https://www.cse.iitd.ernet.in/~aseth/research_associate_position_eo.pdf). Interested software engineers seeking full-time employment, please see this [job description](https://core-stack.org/openings-software-engineer-geospatial-technologies/). This is the place to be if you are eager to make a meaningful change in the world with your work.*
