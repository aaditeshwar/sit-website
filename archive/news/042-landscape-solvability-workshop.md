# Landscape Solvability Workshop

We just concluded an amazing three-day (Nov 5-7th, 2024) workshop to discuss data and tools that can be placed in the hands of community stewards to *solve*for their landscape. With over 50 participants from 20 organizations who engaged in six working session breakouts, the insights and ideas that flowed were extraordinary. We looked over several CoRE stack datasets and discussed their usage and limitations, built decision logics on them to identify common socio-ecological patterns to solve for, built templates for story-lines that use data to suggest actions, and learning tools to equip community stewards to build up their technical understanding on natural resource management concepts.

The agenda and presentations, breakout activities, etc. are linked from [here](http://core-stack.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/know-your-landscape-oct-2024.pdf). We will shortly post a follow-on report on the key findings.
