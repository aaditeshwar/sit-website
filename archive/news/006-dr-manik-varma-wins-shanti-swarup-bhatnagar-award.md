# Dr. Manik Varma wins Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award

Dr. Manik Varma wins Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award in engineering sciences for the year 2019 for his contributions to Machine Learning and Computer Vision. Dr. Varma is an adjunct faculty member at SIT and a distinguished researcher at Microsoft, and has supervised several PhD students at SIT. Read about his work on Extreme Classification that won the Best Paper Award at WSDM'19:
