As more of life runs on software, networks and AI, failures and attacks have consequences in the physical world. Work in this area makes digital systems **secure, private, verifiable and accountable**, and designs them so that compliance with laws such as India's DPDP Act is built in rather than bolted on.

## Featured work

### Network security and privacy (SPRING)

The Security and Privacy Innovation Group focuses on three thrusts:

1. **ML/AI-based intrusion detection** for diverse cyber-physical environments, including smart homes, automotive systems, drones, speech-recognition systems, 5G networks and edge computing. It analyses traffic, device behaviour and user interaction to spot malicious activity.
2. **Efficient and secure authentication** for resource-constrained networked devices, using lightweight cryptography that protects integrity and authenticity without hurting performance.
3. **Privacy-preserving techniques** that protect sensitive data while still allowing it to be authenticated.

![Server racks beside a network switch and cables](/images/research/safe-and-responsible-computing-1.jpg)

**Lead faculty:** [Vireshwar Kumar](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~viresh/) · [spring.iitd.ac.in](https://spring.iitd.ac.in/)

### Federated data discovery

Data-driven decisions power science, business and policy. Regulations such as the GDPR and India's DPDP Act, however, often prevent data from being centralised. When data stays distributed across organisations, new search and discovery mechanisms are needed: decentralised techniques, privacy-preserving search algorithms and intelligent indexing that give access while ensuring compliance.

![Federated data discovery](/images/research/safe-and-responsible-computing-2.png)

**Lead faculty:** [Kaustubh Beedkar](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~kbeedkar/)

### Compliant data systems

Organisations must manage vast amounts of data efficiently while complying with the GDPR, the DPDP Act and the CCPA on access, retention and processing. Existing data systems were not designed with compliance in mind. This work makes **compliance a first-class citizen** of data management, with new ways to specify, enforce and optimise compliance constraints across the data life cycle.

![Compliant data systems](/images/research/safe-and-responsible-computing-3.png)

**Lead faculty:** [Kaustubh Beedkar](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~kbeedkar/)
