## The problem

Smart homes, vehicles, drones, power grids and 5G edge infrastructure connect to the internet faster than they can be secured, and quantum computers threaten today's cryptography.

This cannot be solved in the lab alone. Solutions have to fit real products and real networks.

The hardware side of this work is [Computer architecture](/impact/computer-architecture).

## What we do

The **Security and Privacy Innovation Group (SPRING)** builds ML-based intrusion detection for cyber-physical systems, lightweight authentication for constrained devices, and privacy-preserving data techniques. Other faculty work on **post-quantum cryptography** for network infrastructure, **device fingerprinting** for cyber-physical systems, **anonymity systems and online-fraud defences**, **formal verification** of distributed systems, and **AI-guided code analysis** for safety and security.

The **Centre of Excellence in Cyber Systems and Information Assurance (CoE-CSIA)**, founded in 2014, brings together 22 faculty from Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Management Studies, Mathematics, and Civil and Mechanical Engineering. It works with international partners including the University of Maryland, Newcastle University, Kyushu University and Tallinn University of Technology. SIT hosts the interdisciplinary **M.Tech. in Cybersecurity** with CoE-CSIA.
