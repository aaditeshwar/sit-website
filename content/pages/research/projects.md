# Projects

Explore what SIT faculty and students are working on. Filter by thrust area or by **status**:

- **Ongoing:** active work
- **Open for students:** seeking PhD, MS (Research), pre-doctoral (Research Fellow) or RA candidates
- **Proposed:** new ideas seeking collaborators or funding
- **Completed:** finished work

Interested in a project that is open for students? Write to the faculty contact listed. If you do not yet meet admission criteria, consider the [Research Fellows](/academics/research-fellows) pathway.
