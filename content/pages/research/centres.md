# Centres of Excellence

Centres of Excellence are how SIT works with industry and government over the long term, around shared problems, multi-year roadmaps and projects selected on merit.

## R-Systems CoE for Applied AI for Sustainable Systems

The R-Systems Centre of Excellence focuses on using AI and data-centric strategies to tackle real-world problems. Its aim is to develop responsible and sustainable computational and socio-economic systems, built on cutting-edge information technologies and AI.

**How it works.** A committee of SIT faculty, a representative of R-Systems and external experts calls for and selects project proposals from SIT faculty and their collaborators at least once a year. The CoE supports one or more research projects each year over its five-year term.
