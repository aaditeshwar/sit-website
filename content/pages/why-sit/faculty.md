# Support for faculty and teams

SIT supports its **core, joint and associated faculty** along the whole research-to-impact path: identifying problems, finding collaborators, securing external funding, and deploying and disseminating the results. [Our impact model →](/about/how-we-create-impact)

## IGNITE seed grants

**IGNITE** (Inter-disciplinary Grants for Novel Ideas, Translation and Exploration) supports ambitious, high-risk applied-computing ideas that have clear societal relevance but are not yet mature enough for external funding or need support to tie into larger ongoing initiatives.

- **Who:** teams of at least two PIs, led by an SIT joint faculty member. Collaboration across departments and with external institutions is strongly encouraged.
- **What we look for:** a practical interdisciplinary problem, an innovative applied-computing solution, a real technical or translational risk, and a well-envisioned use-case. Proof-of-concept work and early field pilots are both in scope.
- **What it funds:** flexible project costs such as student and staff hiring, consumables, data collection and field validation, compute, and collaboration, initially for up to two years with an annual review. SIT may also offer workspace, shared infrastructure and administrative support.
- **What we ask:** a short proposal, a clear near-term measure of progress, a strategy for seeking external funding, and a short video for the School's channels.

## Research-to-Impact Collaboration (RIC) workshops

Once a year, SIT convenes colleagues to **discover one another's work and join hands on a larger mission**, in an organic, bottom-up manner. Anyone can propose a session:

- **Project sessions:** a new idea with a long-term vision, and the expertise or resources it needs.
- **Initiative sessions:** mature work looking for colleagues to *take ownership* and expand the work in new directions.
- **Ecosystem sessions:** initiatives at scale, with a strategic roadmap for deeper integration with government, civil society or industry.

Workshops are open to the whole Institute and the wider network of partners. They can lead to seed funding and collaboration grants.

## Yearly research support

Core, joint, associated faculty and Professors of Practice receive **yearly research support**, which can be carried over, for conference registration, travel and publication costs.

## Workshops and events

The School provides partial support for workshops organised by its core, joint and associated faculty on themes relevant to SIT, including events held outside of IIT Delhi.

## Staff for initiatives at scale

For initiatives operating at scale, SIT supports the recruitment of **shared partnerships and programme staff**. They can represent the initiative to government departments and at conferences, reach out to potential donors and organise workshops.

## Research Fellows for your project

Float a [Research Fellow](/academics/research-fellows) position under a well-defined project to bring capable pre-doctoral candidates onto your team.
