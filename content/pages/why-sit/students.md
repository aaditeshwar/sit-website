# Support for students

## Fellowships on top of the institute assistantship

Full-time PhD and MS (Research) scholars are eligible to apply for Fellowships that provide a **monthly stipend over and above their institute assistantship**, for the normal duration of their programme. Selections are made by a selection committee and scholars remain eligible provided they maintain good academic standing. 

## Your own research budget

Every SIT PhD and MS (Research) scholar has a **dedicated research budget for the duration of the programme**. It can be used for conference registration, travel and publication costs. It also covers **workshops and summer or winter schools**, even when you are not presenting a peer-reviewed paper.

## A pathway in: Research Fellows

Not yet through the usual admission route? The [Research Fellows programme](/academics/research-fellows) offers a **funded 6–12 month research position, with no GATE requirement**, leading to PhD or MS (Research) admission. 

## Other fellowships and recognition

SIT scholars have won the **Prime Minister's Research Fellowship**, the **Google PhD Fellowship**, the **FITT Award** for the best industry-relevant thesis and the **Batch of 1969 Innovation Fellow Award**. [News →](/news?category=achievement)
