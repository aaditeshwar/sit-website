# Support for students

Living costs around Delhi are high, and on-campus housing for research scholars is limited. We don't want either to stand between a talented researcher and the work they could do here. SIT therefore supports its scholars **beyond what the Institute provides**.

## School top-up on top of the institute assistantship

Eligible full-time PhD and MS (Research) scholars receive a **monthly School support payment over and above their institute assistantship or project fellowship**, for the normal duration of their programme. Scholars remain eligible while they maintain good academic standing. <!-- VERIFY: approval status of the PhD and MS(R) honorarium policies -->

## Your own research budget

Every SIT PhD and MS (Research) scholar has a **dedicated research budget for the duration of the programme**. It can be used for conference registration, travel and publication costs. It also covers **workshops and summer or winter schools**, even when you are not presenting a peer-reviewed paper, with a supporting note from your supervisor.

## Help with accommodation

When on-campus housing is not available in your first year, the School offers **accommodation support** to help newly admitted MS (Research) and PhD scholars find suitable housing nearby. <!-- VERIFY: scheme details finalised? -->

## A pathway in: Research Fellows

Not yet through the usual admission route? The [Research Fellows programme](/academics/research-fellows) offers a **funded 6–12 month research position, with no GATE requirement**, leading to PhD or MS (Research) admission. For part-time scholars, the School may also extend support beyond the period covered by the supervisor. <!-- VERIFY wording -->
