# MS (Research) programme

The MS (Research) is a **research-oriented master's programme** with lighter coursework and a substantial research thesis. It is an excellent way to experience the excitement of research, often on a live project with real partners, before deciding on a PhD. **Conversion from the MS (Research) to the PhD is common and encouraged at SIT.**

## Support

- **Institute assistantship** for full-time scholars, with **School support on top of the assistantship** for eligible scholars in good academic standing.
- A **dedicated research budget** for each scholar over the programme, for conference registration, travel and publication costs.
- **Accommodation support** in the first year when on-campus housing is not available.

[More on student support →](/why-sit/students)

## Admission

- Interviews are held **twice a year**. Applications are made through the IIT Delhi admissions portal.
- Admission is a two-stage process: **shortlisting**, then **interviews**.
- The [Research Fellows](/academics/research-fellows) programme offers a funded pathway into the MS (Research) as well.

For programme rules, see the latest IIT Delhi [Courses of Study](https://home.iitd.ac.in/curriculum.php).
