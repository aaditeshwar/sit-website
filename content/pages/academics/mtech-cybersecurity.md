# M.Tech. in Cybersecurity

SIT hosts IIT Delhi's interdisciplinary **M.Tech. programme in Cybersecurity**, which admitted its first batch in 2021. The programme brings together the Departments of **Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Management Studies**, so graduates understand security as a technical, mathematical and organisational challenge.
