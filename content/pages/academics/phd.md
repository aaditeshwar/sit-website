# PhD programme

Do a PhD that changes something. Around **80 PhD scholars** (graduated and enrolled) have worked at SIT on interdisciplinary, application-oriented problems in computing. Their work ranges from deep learning for breast-cancer screening in India and accessible mathematics for visually impaired readers to hardware accelerators and satellite-based monitoring of development. <!-- VERIFY current count (old site: ~79) -->

## Who should apply

Because our research crosses disciplines, we welcome candidates from **engineering and many other backgrounds**, and the admission process and the programme itself take this diversity into account. What matters most is a strong foundation, curiosity, and a wish to see your research used.

## Support

- **Institute assistantship** for full-time scholars, with **School support on top of the assistantship** for eligible scholars in good academic standing.
- Many scholars hold **prestigious fellowships** from government and industry, such as the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF) and the Google PhD Fellowship.
- A **dedicated research budget** for each scholar over the programme, for conference registration, travel and publication costs, over and above IIT Delhi's own support, plus support for workshops and summer and winter schools.
- **Accommodation support** in the first year when on-campus housing is not available.

[More on student support →](/why-sit/students)

## Admission

- Interviews are held **twice a year**. Applications are made through the IIT Delhi admissions portal, and are also accepted **throughout the year**: if the portal is closed, request a PDF application form from the SIT office.
- Admission is a two-stage process: **shortlisting**, then **interviews**.
- Not yet ready? The [Research Fellows](/academics/research-fellows) programme offers a funded pathway into the PhD.

For programme rules, see the latest IIT Delhi [Courses of Study](https://home.iitd.ac.in/curriculum.php).
