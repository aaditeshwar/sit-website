# Admissions

## How to apply

Apply through the **Admissions** section of the [IIT Delhi website](https://home.iitd.ac.in/).

| Programme | When | Process |
|---|---|---|
| **PhD** | Throughout the year. If the portal is closed, ask the SIT office for a PDF form. Interviews twice a year. | Shortlisting, then interviews |
| **MS (Research)** | Twice a year | Shortlisting, then interviews |
| **M.Tech. in Cybersecurity** | Once a year | Through the COAP portal |
| **Research Fellows** | Rolling, against specific project positions | [See the programme](/academics/research-fellows) |

## Before you apply

- Browse [projects open to students](/research/projects?status=open-for-students) and the [faculty](/people/faculty) working in your area of interest. Writing to a potential supervisor about a specific project is encouraged.
- Read about the [support SIT offers its scholars](/why-sit/students).
- See the [FAQ](/academics/faq).
