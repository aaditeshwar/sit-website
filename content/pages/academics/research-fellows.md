# Research Fellows programme

**Talented, but not yet through the usual admission route?** SIT's Preparatory Research Fellows programme lets capable candidates join a live research project first, and then apply for the full-time **PhD** or **MS (Research)**.

```yaml key-facts
- { label: "Duration", value: "6–12 months" }
- { label: "Position", value: "Funded, RA-like" }
- { label: "GATE", value: "Not required" }
- { label: "Leads to", value: "PhD or MS (Research) admission" }
```

## How it works

1. **Find a project.** Faculty float specific research-fellow positions under well-defined projects. Browse [projects open to students](/research/projects?status=open-for-students) and look for **Pre-doc welcome**.
2. **Apply** for that position and contact the faculty mentor. You must also fill in the [Research Fellows form](https://forms.gle/Fgrjtj4cxAhGHa79A).
3. **Do research.** Join the team, learn the research process, and aim to publish.
4. **Get admitted.** At the end of the fellowship you can be admitted to the PhD or MS (Research) programme on your mentor's recommendation and after a selection interview.

The fellowship experience can also count towards the experience required for part-time registration.
