# Frequently asked questions

```yaml faq
- q: "I don't have a computer science degree. Can I apply?"
  a: "Yes. SIT's research is interdisciplinary and we welcome candidates from engineering and many other backgrounds. The admission process takes this diversity into account."
- q: "Do I need a GATE score?"
  a: "Programme eligibility follows IIT Delhi rules (see the Courses of Study). The Research Fellows pathway has no GATE requirement."
- q: "When can I apply for the PhD?"
  a: "Applications are accepted throughout the year. Interviews are held twice a year. If the online portal is closed, ask the SIT office for a PDF application form."
- q: "Can I do a PhD or MS (Research) part-time?"
  a: "Yes, both programmes have part-time options. Shortlisting criteria for part-time candidates are published with each admission cycle."
- q: "Is financial support available?"
  a: "Full-time scholars receive the institute assistantship. Eligible SIT scholars also receive School support on top of it, a research budget for conference travel and publications, and accommodation support in their first year when on-campus housing is not available."
- q: "Should I contact a faculty member before applying?"
  a: "It helps. Look at projects open to students and write to the faculty contact about a specific project."
- q: "Can I switch from MS (Research) to the PhD?"
  a: "Yes, conversion is common and encouraged at SIT."
```
