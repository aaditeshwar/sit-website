# Frequently asked questions

```yaml faq
- q: "I don't have a computer science degree. Can I apply?"
  a: "Yes. SIT's research is interdisciplinary and we welcome candidates from engineering and many other backgrounds. The admission process takes this diversity into account but please ensure that you contact relevant faculty working in your areas of interest especially if you come from a non-CS/IT/AI/EE/Maths background."
- q: "Do I need a GATE score?"
  a: "Programme eligibility follows IIT Delhi rules (see the Courses of Study). The Research Fellows pathway has no GATE requirement."
- q: "When can I apply for the PhD?"
  a: "Applications are accepted throughout the year. Interviews are held twice a year. If the online portal is closed, ask the SIT office for a PDF offline application form."
- q: "Can I do a PhD or MS (Research) part-time?"
  a: "Yes, both programmes have part-time options. Shortlisting criteria for part-time candidates are published with each admission cycle."
- q: "Is financial support available?"
  a: "Full-time scholars receive the institute assistantship. Eligible SIT scholars also receive School fellowships on top of it, and a research budget for conference travel and publications."
- q: "Should I contact a faculty member before applying?"
  a: "It helps. Look at projects open to students and write to the faculty contact about a specific project."
- q: "Can I switch from MS (Research) to the PhD?"
  a: "Yes, conversion is common and encouraged at SIT."
- q: "Who do I contact with questions?"
  a: "SIT office email (eoaesit[@]admin.iitd.ac.in) or phone (+91 11 2659 6056)."
```
