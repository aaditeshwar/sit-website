# Who we are

The **Amar Nath and Shashi Khosla School of Information Technology (SIT)** was established in 2004 under the aegis of IIT Delhi, with an endowment from the distinguished alumnus **Vinod Khosla** (B.Tech., Electrical Engineering, 1976). Its purpose is to foster interdisciplinary, goal-oriented research, innovation and postgraduate education in applied computing.

SIT is not a conventional department. It is a **meeting ground**. Faculty hold joint, associated and adjunct appointments, so the School draws together expertise from computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical and product design, biomedical engineering, chemistry, management, the humanities, and partners such as AIIMS, Microsoft and STMicroelectronics. What unites them is a shared commitment to **work that leaves the lab**: research with a clear path to deployment, built in collaboration, and woven into the ecosystems it aims to change.

## What we do

- **Research with purpose.** We focus on four thrust areas where computing can make a decisive difference: sustainability and social development, sustainable and scalable computing, health and social care, and safe and responsible computing. [Explore our research →](/research/projects)
- **Take ideas to scale.** Several SIT efforts have grown from student projects into products, platforms and organisations used by millions of people. [See our impact →](/impact/core-stack)
- **Train researchers who create change.** Our PhD, MS (Research) and M.Tech. programmes, and a preparatory Research Fellows pathway, welcome students from engineering and many other backgrounds. [Academics →](/academics)
- **Invest in people.** The School's endowment funds student support, research travel, seed grants, collaboration workshops and programme staff. [Why SIT →](/why-sit)
