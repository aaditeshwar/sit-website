# Vision

> **The School shall promote, facilitate and provide resources for research in applied computing with a strong interdisciplinary focus, and with an eye towards societal impact.**

Computing is becoming central to every discipline. SIT exists so that this shift creates real value for society: water-secure villages, accessible classrooms, earlier cancer detection, greener data centres and trustworthy AI. We pursue this through research that has a clear path to deployment, through collaborations that turn single projects into lasting initiatives, and through deep engagement with the ecosystems we hope to transform. ## Programmes

The School offers full-time (funded) and part-time **PhD** and **MS (Research)** programmes, the interdisciplinary **M.Tech. in Cybersecurity**, and a funded **Research Fellows** programme that prepares candidates for admission to the PhD and MS (Research) programmes.
